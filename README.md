# Welcome！


This is a small Survivors-like game made with Unity. 

You play as a vampire wielding various weapons to fend off enemies. The goal is simple: survive as long as possible.


## Overview


This is my first attempt at creating a Survivors-like game. The commit history documents my step-by-step progress in developing it. You can find more detailed information by clicking [here](https://www.udemy.com/course/unity-vampire-survival/?couponCode=LETSLEARNNOWPP)


## Gameplay

You can use WASD on your keyboard to control your character's movements. 

There are UIs on the screen that show you how much health, gold and level you have. 

Enemies will come at you from any direction, and all you have to do is attack them with your weapons. 

When an enemy dies, it will drop experience and gold, experience can make you level up, when you level up, you can use coins on the upgrade panel to strengthen your weapons, buy new weapons or improve your character's attributes.
